> Last updated: March 2026. Re-research every 6 months.

## Market Overview

**Total US SAT takers annually:** ~1.7 million  
**Market structure:** Crowded at the surface, wide open underneath.  
**Key insight:** Every existing platform has built a test bank with a chatbot. Nobody has built structured pedagogical AI.

## The 8 Players

### 1. Khan Academy + Khanmigo
**Price:** Free / $4/month for AI  
**Positioning:** Official College Board partner. Free, trusted.

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Official CB questions — licensed, reliable | No full adaptive exam engine (Module 1 → 2) |
| Khanmigo uses Socratic questioning | AI made basic math errors (WSJ test, 2024) |
| Google Gemini integration (Jan 2026) | No score prediction or test analytics |
| Free parent dashboard | General purpose — not SAT-specific AI |
| Nonprofit — no sales pressure | No structured pedagogical tabs |

**Verdict:** Biggest free competitor. Khanmigo is Socratic but unstructured. Our 3-tab system is more pedagogically rigorous.

## The 5 Market Gaps Nobody Has Filled

### Gap 1 — Structured Pedagogical AI
Every platform: one chat box. "Ask me anything."  
Nobody has: Three distinct AI perspectives (Solution, Concept, Prerequisites) per question.

**This is your strongest differentiator.**

### Gap 2 — Interactive AI Modes
Nobody has: Debate Mode, Error Autopsy, Socratic Hints, What-If Explorer.  
The entire market is passive consumption. You're building active engagement.

### Gap 3 — Exam Mode vs Review Mode Separation
Nobody has made this a design principle. AI during exam practice is bad for learning. Nobody enforces this.

### Gap 4 — NRI and Indian Diaspora Student
Zero platforms target DASA pathway, JEE+SAT overlap, Indian-American community specifically.

### Gap 5 — Affordable + Premium AI Quality
Free = weak AI. $99+ = better content but circular AI.  
$14.99 with better AI architecture than the $99 platform = unserved gap.
