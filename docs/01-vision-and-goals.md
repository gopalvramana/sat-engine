# 01 — Vision and Goals

---

## The Problem We're Solving

Every existing SAT platform shows you the answer. None of them teach you why you got it wrong. A student who chose the wrong trig ratio and a student who made a sign error see the exact same explanation. That is not tutoring. That is a textbook with a search function.

The three-tab AI panel changes this. Solution explains how to solve this specific problem. Core Concept explains the underlying mathematical idea. Prerequisites diagnoses what the student needs to learn before they can do this type of problem again.

---

## What We Are Building

**An AI tutoring platform with a SAT exam engine** — not a test bank with a chatbot bolted on.

The exam engine is the delivery mechanism. The AI panel is the product. The question bank is the inventory.

### Two Modes — A Core Design Principle

| Mode | AI Available | Timer | Purpose |
|------|-------------|-------|---------|
| **Exam Mode** | No | Yes | Simulate the real SAT. Build genuine skill. |
| **Review Mode** | Yes (full panel) | No | Learn from every wrong answer. |

This separation is intentional. The real SAT has no AI. Practicing with AI during a timed test builds dependency, not skill. AI during review is where real learning happens — and where our cost model works.

## Who We're Building For

### Primary Target
Any motivated SAT student scoring **1100–1350** targeting **1400+**.

- Motivated enough to pay for a tool
- Has conceptual gaps (not just practice gaps) — the AI panel helps them
- Price sensitive — our $14.99/month vs Acely's $99–199/month matters
- Disproportionately includes Indian-American students (our warm launch community)

### Launch Channel vs Permanent Identity
Indian-American community = warm launch channel for first 30–50 users. Not a permanent identity or ceiling.

**Total addressable market: 1.7 million US SAT takers annually.**

At 0.1% (1,700 students): **$25,000/month**. That is a Year 3 realistic target.

### Who We Are NOT Building For
- Students below 900 who need foundational academic help
- Students already above 1450 who just need more practice volume
- Students looking for the cheapest possible option (Khan Academy is free)
