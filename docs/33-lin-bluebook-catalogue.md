## 4. Recommendations

### Significant gap — add (high priority, 6 BB questions)
- **`graph_to_inequality` template (d1/d2):** A coordinate plane with a shaded half-plane is shown. Student reads the boundary line (slope and y-intercept) and shading direction, then selects the correct inequality. Easy variant: horizontal boundary (y = c), student picks y ≤ c or y ≥ c. Medium variant: sloped boundary, student must identify slope, intercept, and shading direction together. This requires a diagram — the generator must produce a `coordinate_plane` diagram with a boundary line and shading indicator.

### Weak anchor — review
- **`two_var_bound_neg`:** No Bluebook question explicitly asks the student to "divide both sides of an inequality by a negative number and flip the sign." The template may still be pedagogically valid (sign-flip is a key skill), but it is not directly anchored to an observed question. Consider either: (a) confirming it produces questions that match the existing `two_var_bound` / `table_satisfies` patterns at d3, or (b) deprecating it and redirecting to `table_satisfies_twovar`.

### No action needed
All other 17 templates have confirmed BB anchors and reasonable frequency.
