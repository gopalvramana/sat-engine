## 4. Recommendations

### Add (medium priority — 4 questions each in Bluebook)
- **"Classify number of solutions" template (d2/d3):** Given a linear equation with distribution on both sides, the student expands, collects terms, and classifies as exactly one / no solution / infinitely many. Distinct from the current "find parameter for no/infinite solutions" pattern — here the equation is fully specified and student classifies it. Could be a new d2 template `classify_solutions`.
- **"Select modeling equation" template (d1/d2 word problem variant):** Student reads a word problem and selects the equation that encodes it, without solving. Cognitively distinct from `direct_solve_*`. Could be a d1 variant of `equivalent_equation` or a standalone template.

### Structural note
~40% of Bluebook L1V questions are word problems. These are intentionally handled by the LWP skill. Confirm that sessions show both L1V and LWP questions to students — otherwise a significant category of SAT questions is invisible.
